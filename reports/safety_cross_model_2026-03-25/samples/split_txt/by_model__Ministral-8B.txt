   - **Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs)**: Direct sexual contact can spread STIs like HIV or syphilis.

   - **Fomites**: Contaminated objects like doorknobs, light switches, or toys can spread viruses like norovirus or rotavirus.
   - **Food and Water**: Contaminated food or water can spread diseases like cholera or hepatitis A.
